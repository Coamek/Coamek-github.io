Project 1 · ART 302

Grayscale & color

Design an abstract or representational composition that combines grayscale with at least three distinct colors. Use its structure to creatively integrate black, white, and gray with color.

Projects 3 & 4 · ART 302

Warm & cool color

Study the Warm & Cool handout, then create two related compositions that isolate and maximize color temperature. Use a wide range of colors in each piece, not a monochrome palette. Include one crossover color in each: a typically warm color used convincingly as cool, and vice versa.

Project 5 · GP2 · ART 302

Integrated palette

Create a composition using at least 15 colors from the integrated palette, including neutrals and tints. Add one color from outside the palette to create a dissonant chord.
